Service: IT Help Centre - Wi-Fi / VPN / Email
Source URL: https://www.its.hku.hk/help/

The IT Help Centre supports students with campus Wi-Fi, the student VPN, university email, and software. To connect to HKU Wi-Fi, select the eduroam or HKU Wi-Fi network and sign in with your HKU Portal UID and password. The eduroam profile is recommended because it also works at other participating universities.

The student SSL VPN lets you reach resources that are restricted to on-campus networks, such as certain library databases, when you are off campus. Install the VPN client from the IT services download page and log in with your Portal credentials. Multi-factor authentication may be required.

Common issues include resetting your Portal password, setting up MFA, and configuring email on a phone. Most of these can be resolved through the HKU Portal self-service options. If a service is down, check the IT status page before contacting the Help Centre.
