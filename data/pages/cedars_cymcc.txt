Chong Yuet Ming Cultural Centre (CYMCC) houses two performing venues and other peripheral facilities : Yuet Ming Auditorium and Studio 303; Rehearsal Room; Student Common Room cum Library, Exhibition and Busking areas.
Event
CYMCC was in full operation since July, 2016. Since then, there are over 250 events of various natures capturing over 60000 attendees held in the performing venues in CYMCC.
Our Mission
CYMCC not only provides good quality facility for the art and cultural performances in the University but also served as an enabling platform for the students to get engaged in the theatrical operation and cultural exposure.
