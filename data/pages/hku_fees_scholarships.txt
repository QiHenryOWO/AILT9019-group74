AIA Scholarships
The Scholarships shall be awarded to local full-time Year 1 undergraduate students , primarily on academic merit and participation in community services or charitable work. Where candidates are of equal merit, priority will be given to students from families experiencing financial difficulty and who would not otherwise have the opportunity to pursue a university education.

The Hong Kong Jockey Club Compassionate Leader Scholarship
The Hong Kong Jockey Club Undergraduate Scholarship aims to recognise outstanding students for their intellect, character, leadership qualities and commitment to service. Under the Hong Kong Jockey Club Scholarships – Undergraduate Scholarship scheme, up to three scholarships are awarded to local students from HKU each year effective August 2023.
