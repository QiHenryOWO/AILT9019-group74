HKU Portal Account
HKU Portal is a single sign-on platform hosting most of the central IT services and applications offered by University departments. Staff and students will be provided with an HKU Portal account (HKU Portal UID (User Identification) and an associated PIN (Personal Identification Number) for accessing HKU Portal.
