Service: CEDARS - Student Card & Octopus
Source URL: https://www.cedars.hku.hk/student.html

Your HKU student card is your official identity on campus. It functions as your library card, gives access to halls and sport facilities, and is used for examinations and borrowing equipment. The card can be linked to an Octopus function so you can make small payments in canteens, printers, and campus shops.

If your card is lost or stolen, report it immediately through the HKU Portal to suspend it and prevent misuse; a replacement card can be issued for a fee. Keep a record of your card number so suspension is faster.

For Octopus top-up and balance enquiries, use the Octopus app, Add Value Machines on campus, or convenience stores. The student card and the Octopus balance are managed separately, so suspending the student card does not automatically refund or freeze the Octopus value.
