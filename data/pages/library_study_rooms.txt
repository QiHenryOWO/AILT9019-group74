Service: HKU Libraries - Study Rooms & Facilities
Source URL: https://lib.hku.hk/services/

HKU Libraries provide group study rooms and individual quiet study spaces across the Main Library and the branch libraries. Group study rooms can be booked online through the library booking system using your HKU Portal account. Bookings are typically limited to a few hours per session and can be made up to one week in advance. You must check in at a self-service kiosk or online within a short grace period after the booking starts, otherwise the room is automatically released for others to use.

The library also offers laptop and equipment loans, printing and scanning facilities, and extended or 24-hour study areas during examination periods. Silent study floors are clearly signposted and mobile phones should be set to silent. For help, contact the Loans and Enquiries counter on the ground floor of the Main Library, or use the library live-chat service during opening hours.

If you cannot find a free group room, some branches operate a drop-in group study zone that does not require booking. Always bring your student card, since it is required to enter the library and to borrow equipment.
