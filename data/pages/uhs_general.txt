Service: University Health Service (UHS)
Source URL: https://www.uhs.hku.hk/

The University Health Service provides primary medical care to registered HKU students. Services include general medical consultations, women's health, travel health advice, and referrals to specialists when needed. Appointments can be made online through the UHS booking portal or by telephone during office hours.

UHS is a general practice clinic and is not an emergency service. For any medical emergency, call 999 for an ambulance or go to the nearest Accident and Emergency department. Consultation fees for students are heavily subsidised, and some basic medications are dispensed on site.

If you feel unwell but it is not an emergency, book a routine appointment rather than going to A&E, which should be reserved for life-threatening conditions. UHS also offers health education talks on topics such as sleep, nutrition, and managing stress during exams.
