Service: CEDARS - Accommodation Service
Source URL: https://www.cedars.hku.hk/acc

CEDARS helps students find off-campus accommodation and maintains a database of registered flat shares, shared rooms, and verified landlords. On-campus halls of residence are managed separately by the Hall Administration Office and are allocated through a different process.

The CEDARS accommodation service offers tenancy guidance, a searchable list of available rooms, and advice on rental contracts, deposits, and tenants' rights under Hong Kong law. Students who are new to Hong Kong are strongly advised to attend an accommodation briefing before signing any lease. Typical pitfalls include paying a deposit before viewing the flat and unclear utility arrangements.

If you experience a dispute with a landlord, CEDARS can provide mediation support and refer you to independent tenancy advice. Never transfer a large deposit to someone you have not met in person, and always keep written records of agreements.
