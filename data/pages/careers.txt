Service: Careers and Placement Centre
Source URL: https://careers.hku.hk/

The Careers and Placement Centre helps students with career planning, resume and cover letter review, mock interviews, and finding jobs and internships. Students can book one-to-one career advising appointments and browse the online jobs board for vacancies and internship postings.

Throughout the semester the centre runs workshops on networking, assessment centres, case interviews, and applications for graduate school. Employer information sessions and career fairs are advertised on the centre's portal and by email.

If you are unsure about your career direction, start with a career advising appointment rather than only applying to postings. The centre can also advise on work rights and practical training arrangements for international students. Keep your profile and CV on the jobs board up to date so employers can find you.
