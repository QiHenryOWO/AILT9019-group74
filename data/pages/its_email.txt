Email and Calendar
Email and calendar service is provided for use by all University members. It is supported by both in-house email systems and outsourced email platform with anti-spam and anti-virus protection.
Supported by a number of in-house systems running on Microsoft Exchange (HKUCC1 or HKUCC-COM@hku.hk). Each account is allocated with 15GB disk quota (email archive is available when disk quota exceeds) with accessibility to Global Address List (GAL).
