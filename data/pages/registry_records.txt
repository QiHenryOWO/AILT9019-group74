Service: Registry - Academic Records & Transcripts
Source URL: https://www.reg.hku.hk/

The Registry is responsible for academic records, including official transcripts, enrollment certificates, and the academic calendar. Current students can order an official transcript online through the HKU Portal; it can be delivered electronically as a certified PDF or by post in a sealed envelope.

An enrollment certificate, confirming your current student status, is commonly required for visa extensions, bank accounts, or scholarships. You can generate this yourself from the Portal without visiting the Registry in person.

Grade release dates and the add/drop deadlines are published in the academic calendar each term. If you need a document in a hurry, check the Portal first because many certificates are now self-service. For special requests such as a letter explaining your study load, contact the Registry with your student number ready.
