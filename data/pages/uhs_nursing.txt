Vaccination Services
Vaccines under the Hong Kong Childhood Immunisation Programme, the Vaccination Subsidy Scheme, travel health, and other categories are available. Patients are advised to enquire about the vaccine availability prior to booking. Doctor’s consultation is required before administration.
Some vaccines require advance ordering and processing time. Please schedule your visit accordingly to allow sufficient lead time.
Vaccination fees are charged at cost and are non-refundable.
Note: Some patients may experience discomfort after vaccination. To allow sufficient time for post-vaccination observation, please arrive at the clinic by 5:00 pm.
