Service: CEDARS - Financial Assistance
Source URL: https://www.cedars.hku.hk/financial

CEDARS (Centre of Development and Resources for Students) offers financial assistance to students in demonstrated need. Support includes means-tested grants, interest-free emergency loans, and a range of scholarships and bursaries. Emergency loans are intended for unexpected short-term financial difficulty and are normally repaid by instalments from your bank account or through arrangements with the Bursary.

To apply, you usually need to submit a statement of financial circumstances together with supporting documents such as bank statements or proof of unexpected expenses. Students facing sudden hardship, for example a family income shock, should contact CEDARS as early as possible rather than waiting until fees are due. Non-means-tested loans may also be available for specific purposes such as travel for an approved exchange.

CEDARS also runs financial literacy workshops and can advise on budgeting while studying in Hong Kong. All applications are handled in confidence by the CEDARS student welfare team.
