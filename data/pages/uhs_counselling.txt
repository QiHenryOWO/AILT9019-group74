Service: UHS - Counselling Service
Source URL: https://www.uhs.hku.hk/counselling/

The UHS Counselling Service offers confidential psychological support to students dealing with stress, anxiety, low mood, relationship difficulties, or adjustment to university life. Students can self-refer by booking an appointment online through the UHS portal; no referral from a doctor is required.

Counselling is free and confidential, and sessions are conducted by trained professionals. The service also runs group workshops on wellbeing, mindfulness, and stress management throughout the semester.

If you are experiencing an immediate mental-health crisis, or thinking about harming yourself, contact 999 for emergency help, call the Samaritans 24-hour hotline at 2389 2222, or go to the nearest Accident and Emergency department. These are outside the counselling appointment system and are available at any time. The counselling team can also provide follow-up support once you are safe.
