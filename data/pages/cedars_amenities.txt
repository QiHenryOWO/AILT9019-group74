The Centre of Development and Resources for Students (CEDARS) manages three Amenities Centres and the facilities are open for use by students and staff as individuals or in groups. Some facilities can be booked online for exclusive use. Priority is given to students, student societies, and student organized activities.
Each Centre has a Manager who is responsible for the overall management of the facilities. With a team of supporting staff, each Manager runs a booking office and handles all bookings of facilities and other management matters in the Centre.
A continuous process of consultation with students on the management policies and the design and use of facilities has been maintained for years, and will continue. Consultative groups meet regularly for this purpose.
