Charging Policies
The University Health Service (UHS) Main Campus Clinic provides services to a wide range of patient groups. In line with University policies, consultation charges may differ depending on individual users’ eligibility and benefit level.
For HKU Students (please click for details)
The University Health Service (UHS) Main Campus Clinic supports the University in fostering a whole-person approach to education. The UHS Main Campus Clinic offers a range of subsidized primary health care services for eligible students.
For HKU students, general medical consultation is free of charge. Some investigations and medication are provided at a cost.
Medical charges are as follows:
UHS Services
Charges*
Well Woman Check-up

$600

$600
Medical Reports – Driving License Renewal
$600

* The above medical charges do not include any investigations or x-ray which will be charged to the patients.
* All the above medical charges are non-refundable.
Please note that a $100 fee will be charged for each receipt reprint. Patients are reminded to keep their receipts carefully.
Remarks:
The above medical charges are for reference only and may be subject to change. Please kindly check with UHS Main Campus Clinic staff prior to using the service.
For HKU Staff and their Dependants (please click for details)
With effect from 1 September 2026, the University will implement a new Group Medical Insurance Scheme for eligible HKU staff members and their dependants. For information on the new Group Medical Insurance Scheme, please refer to the Bulk Email issued by the Human Resources Office (HRO) on 17 August 2026: Bulk Email [please click here for details].
The UHS Main Campus Clinic will serve as a non-network / non-panel clinic under the University’s Group Medical Insurance Scheme. Eligible HKU staff members and their dependants attending UHS Main Campus Clinic are required to pay for all services at the point of service. These services include, but are not limited to, medical consultations, medications, nursing services, physiotherapy services, and dental services.
As advised by HRO, for all eligible HKU Staff and their dependants, UHS Main Campus Medical Clinic will charge HK$280 per consultation, covering a medical consultation together with up to three days’ supply of basic medication.
