Eligibility to Use UHS Services
Our Services >> Eligibility to Use UHS Services
The UHS Main Campus Clinic only provides services for eligible staff and their dependents, as well as students. If a user becomes
ineligible for UHS Main Campus Clinic services (e.g. completion of employment contract or studies), the user and his/her dependents
will not be able to receive UHS Main Campus Clinic services even if an appointment was booked previously. Any booked appointments
or uncollected medications will be cancelled without prior notice.
All users are advised to ensure that they are eligible before coming to the UHS Main Campus Clinic:
For Students
Students could check with their Faculty or Department concerning their eligibility.
For Staff
Staff members are advised to check with the Human Resources Office for their eligibility.
