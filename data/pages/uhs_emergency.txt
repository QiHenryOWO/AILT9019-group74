For Medical Emergencies:
Please refer to Safety Office’s Response for Medical Emergency
The nearest Accident and Emergency Department to the University of Hong Kong is the Queen Mary Hospital,
which is a public hospital, on Pokfulam Road.
For Dental Emergencies:
We provide same day emergency service for the relief of dental pain and infection if you have:
Severe toothache
Swelling and / or infection with or without fever
Accident involving tooth and jaw
Broken tooth with pain or sensitivity
Oral lesion and any other dental emergency
For other Emergencies:
Please refer to Safety Office’s Action in Case of an Emergency
